# Data and attribution notice

The author-owned public numeric view includes all radar rows and reference
joints for 221 primary and 32 secondary stored records, plus a three-payload ROS
excerpt and its recorded point/reference streams. Array values are retained;
privacy-minimized containers have new file hashes and list original-source
hashes separately. No RGB images, dense reference surfaces, private filesystem
paths, historical checkpoints or personal identifiers are distributed here.

The INRAS retained IF excerpt is attributed to Lukas Rienessl and Reinhard Feger,
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21933926, CC BY 4.0. Its original 76–77-GHz configuration is
preserved. External raw records with an unspecified distinct dataset license
are referenced through their original publisher rather than relicensed.

The human-participant protocol and acquisition setup are author-confirmed
provenance. Configuration statements do not add per-event switch, distance,
CQ or ADC-calibration measurements to saved records. Public time vectors use
an elapsed recording clock after exact origin subtraction.

Software unit-test fixtures are kept in the test suite. They are not empirical
hardware records or additional attack trials. The paper's count, power and
contrast results preserve their original statistical units.
